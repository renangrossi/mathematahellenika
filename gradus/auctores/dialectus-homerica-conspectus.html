Gradus VII · Auctores

Dialectus Homerica: Conspectus

Iōnicum-Aeolicum mixtum artificiāle; genetīvī in -οιο/-εω/-αο; fōrmae uncontractae; digamma perditum; augmentum omissum — nōn errōrēs, sed systēma proprium.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

"Graecum Homericum" nōn est dialectus locūta ab ūllā cīvitāte reālī antīquā (dīversē ab Atticō, Iōnicō, Dōricō, quae erant vērō locūta) — est compositum poēticum artificiāle, aedificātum per generātiōnēs aedōrum orālium (bardōrum) super mīlle annōs, combīnāns praesertim elementa Iōnica cum stratīs Aeolicīs antīquiōribus et fōrmulīs archaicīs cōnservātīs propter metrum — lingua quae numquam exstitit in ūsū cotīdiānō, sed sōlum in trāditiōne epicā.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Explicāre cūr “Graecum Homericum” nōn sit dialectus locūta umquam ab ūllā commūnitāte reālī, sed compositum artificiāle poēticum
  • Recognōscere trēs fōrmās morphologicās praecipuās dīversās ab Atticō: genetīvī -οιο/-εω/-αο, fōrmae uncontractae, dēsinentia datīvī plūrālis -εσσι
  • Explicāre digamma (ϝ), littera perdita cūius effectus phōnologicus (praeveniēns hiātum) manet vīsibilis in metrō, quamquam ipsa littera nōn scrībitur
  • Recognōscere augmentum saepe OMISSUM in Homērō, dīversē ā rēgulā obligātōriā Atticā (Gradū III)

Explicatio

Explicatio

Discipulus quī iam nōvit Atticum (fundāmentum tōtīus huius cursūs) potest legere Homērum cum aliquā difficultāte initiālī, sed māiōrī parte vocābulī et grammaticae RECOGNOSCIBILĪ — discrīmina sunt systēmatica et discibilia, nōn chaotica.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Genetīvī Dīversī

DēclīnātiōAtticumHomericum
2ª (masc./neut.)-ου-οιο
1ª (masc.)-ου-εω / -αο

Exemplum iam vīsum: Πηληϊάδεω (Homericum) = Πηληϊάδου (Atticum, sī exstāret).

b) Fōrmae Uncontractae; Dēsinentia -εσσι

Ubi Atticum contrahit vōcālēs adiacentēs (Gradū III, verba contracta), Homērus saepe RETINET fōrmam uncontractam, metrī grātiā: ἄλγεα (Homericum, III syllabae) vs. ἄλγη (Atticum, contractum, II syllabae) — iam vīsum in Iliade I.2! Dēclīnātiō tertia frequenter adhibet dēsinentiam datīvī plūrālis -εσσι (alternātīva/additiōnālis ad -σι Atticum): ἔπεσσι ("verbīs") = ἔπεσι Atticum.

c) Digamma et Augmentum Omissum

Digamma (ϝ, sonāns "w") erat littera reālis in alphabētō prīmōrdiālī Graecō (Gradū I, mentiōnāta ut littera antīqua dēsuēta in numerālibus), perdita in Atticō classicō ANTE textūs scrīptōs quōs habēmus, sed cūius EFFECTUS PHŌNOLOGICUS manet vīsibilis in metrō Homericō: verba quae "dēbērent" causāre hiātum (duae vōcālēs adiacentēs sine elisiōne) nōn id faciunt, quia digamma (invīsibilis in scrīptūrā, sed adhūc "audīta" metricē) praevenit collīsiōnem. Augmentum (Gradū III, obligātōrium in Atticō) est FREQUENTER OMISSUM in Homērō — flexibilitās metrica permittit utramque fōrmam (cum vel sine augmentō) secundum necessitātem versūs.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • Πηληϊάδεω (Hom.) = Πηληϊάδου (Att.) — genetīvus, dēsinentia -εω
  • ἄλγεα (Hom.) = ἄλγη (Att.) — fōrma uncontracta vs. contracta
  • ἔπεσσι (Hom.) = ἔπεσι (Att.) — datīvus plūrālis, -εσσι alternātīvum
  • ϝοῖκος (“domus”, cum digammate reconstrūctō) — comparā Latīnum “vīcus” (V initiāle Latīnum ↔ digamma Graecum, cognātiō Indoeuropaea vīsibilis!)
  • βάλε (Hom., sine augmentō) = ἔβαλε (Att., cum augmentō) — ambae fōrmae possibilēs apud Homērum

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumCrēdere Graecum Homericum esse simpliciter “Graecum antīquius” quod omnēs Graecī classicī locūtī sunt ante Atticum.

RectiusRecognōscere Homericum esse compositum ARTIFICIĀLE poēticum, numquam locūtum cotīdiānē ab ūllā commūnitāte reālī, distīnctum ā dialectīs reālibus (Iōnicā, Aeolicā, Dōricā) quibus partim dērīvat.

Confūsiō hīc impedit intellēctum nātūrae genuīnae “Graecī Homericī” ut phaenomenon litterārium, nōn sociolinguisticum.

VitiumSuppōnere absentiam augmentī in fōrmā verbālī Homericā indicāre tempus dīversum (ut praesēns prō praeteritō).

RectiusRecognōscere absentiam augmentī hīc esse mēra licentia metrica, sine effectū sēmanticō — fōrma manet praeteritum, sōlum sine signō explicitō.

Flexibilitās metrica Homērica saepe permittit variātiōnem fōrmālem sine variātiōne sēmanticā correspondentī.

VitiumIgnōrāre digamma omnīnō, quia nōn scrībitur in textū standard, frūstrandō sē cūr metrum interdum vidētur “īrregulāre”.

RectiusRecordārī digamma, quamquam invīsibile in scrīptūrā, adhūc “sonat” metricē in multīs verbīs (reconstruendīs per comparātiōnem cum aliīs linguīs Indoeuropaeīs, ut Latīnum v-).

Multa “hiāta apparentia” Homērica dēsōlvuntur completē cum digamma reconstitūtum mente restituitur.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Graecum Homericum est compositum poēticum artificiāle (Iōnicum + Aeolicum + fōrmulae archaicae), numquam locūtum cotīdiānē.
  • Genetīvī dīversī: -οιο (2ª decl.), -εω/-αο (1ª decl. masc.) prō -ου Atticō.
  • Fōrmae uncontractae (ἄλγεα vs. ἄλγη) et dēsinentia -εσσι (datīvus pl., alternātīva) sunt frequentēs.
  • Digamma (ϝ, “w”, perdita) manet vīsibilis phōnologicē in metrō, explicāns hiātūs apparentēs. Augmentum saepe omissum, licentia metrica.
