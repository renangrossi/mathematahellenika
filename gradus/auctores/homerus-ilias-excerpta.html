Gradus VII · Auctores

Homērus: Ilias

Verba authentica: prīmī duo versūs Iliadis (I.1-2) — versūs maximē citātī tōtīus litterātūrae Graecae — praeparātiō ad dialectum Homericam et metrum epicum.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Hic textus est authenticus — prīmī duo versūs (ex XV milibus circiter) Iliadis, epicī antīquissimī et fortasse maximē īnfluentis tōtīus litterātūrae Eurōpaeae, trāditiōnāliter attribūtī "Homērō" (persōna cūius historicitās ipsa incerta manet — vidē lēctiōnem sequentem dē dialectō). Hī versūs sunt inter fōrmulās maximē memorātās omnis studentis Graecī classicī, antīquī et modernī.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Legere et memoriae mandāre prīmōs duōs versūs Iliadis, fortasse versūs maximē cēlebrēs tōtīus litterātūrae antīquae
  • Reconhōscere prīmās fōrmās dialectī Homericae (Epicae/Ioniae) vīsās in hōc textū, praeparāns lēctiōnem sequentem
  • Explicāre thema “īram” (μῆνις) ut argūmentum organizāns tōtīus Iliadis
  • Comparāre invocātiōnem Mūsae Homericam cum aliīs epicīs (Vergiliō Latīnō, quem discipulus fortasse iam nōvit)

Explicatio

Explicatio

Ilias narrat (partem) Bellī Trōiānī, focāta in īrā (μῆνις) Achillis, herōis praecipuī Graecōrum, contrā Agamemnonem, ducem exercitūs — īra quae, per tōtum epicum, causat plūrimās mortēs Graecōrum ipsōrum. Versus prīmus statim nōminat thema (μῆνιν, verbum PRĪMUM tōtīus operis — positiō ēmphatica maximē significāns) et invocat Mūsam (patrōnam poēsis) ad canendum.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

Textus: Ilias I.1-2

μῆνιν ἄειδε θεὰ Πηληϊάδεω Ἀχιλῆος
οὐλομένην, ἣ μυρί᾽ Ἀχαιοῖς ἄλγε᾽ ἔθηκε,

Versiō Latīna: "Īram cane, dea, Achillis Pēlēiadae, / exitiālem, quae innumera Achīvīs mala intulit,"

Commentārius: μῆνιν — accūsātīvus, obiectum verbī ἄειδε, positiō PRĪMA ēmphatica (Gradū VI: ōrdō = focus) — tōtum epicum incipit ipsō themate. ἄειδε — imperātīvus (Gradū V), fōrma Epica prō Atticō ᾄδω. Πηληϊάδεω, Ἀχιλῆος — genetīvī in fōrmā DIALECTĪ (Epica/Iōnica: -εω prō Atticō -ου; -ος prō Atticō -εως) — prīma exposītiō ad dialectum Homericam, quam proximā lēctiōne systēmatice tractābis. οὐλομένην — participium (Gradū IV), "exitiālis", concordāns cum μῆνιν, in appositiōne. ἔθηκε — aoristus verbī τίθημι ("pōnō, faciō") — verbum āthematicum ("-μι"), classis nōn systēmaticē tractāta hōc cursū, sed glōssanda hīc ut vocābulum frequēns.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • μῆνιν ἄειδε θεά. — Īram cane, dea. (invocātiō Mūsae, comparā “Arma virumque canō” Vergiliī!)
  • Πηληϊάδεω Ἀχιλῆος — Achillis Pēlēiadae (patronymicum + nōmen proprium, genetīvus Epicus)
  • μυρία ἄλγεα — innumera mala (accūsātīvus plūrālis neutrum, fōrma uncontracta Epica, comparā ἄλγη Atticum)
  • Ἀχαιοῖς — Achīvīs/Graecīs (datīvus plūrālis, nōmen Graecōrum apud Homērum)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumCrēdere Iliadem incipere nārrātiōne integrā Bellī Trōiānī (ab ovō, ut dīcitur), sīcut modernī expectant ā “epicō”.

RectiusRecognōscere Iliadem incipere IN MEDIĀS RĒS (“in media rē”), focātam in ūnō episōdiō (īra Achillis, ultimō annō bellī), nōn tōtā historia decem annōrum.

Convention epica “in mediās rēs” (ipsa nōmināta ā practicā Homēricā) īnfluit omnem epicum posteriōrem, inclūsō Vergiliō.

VitiumSuppōnere fōrmās dialectī Homericae (Πηληϊάδεω, Ἀχιλῆος) esse errōrēs vel fōrmās “incorrēctās” Atticī quod iam nōvistī.

RectiusRecognōscere dialectum Epicam/Iōnicam esse SYSTĒMA PROPRIUM, antīquius et diversum ab Atticō, nōn corruptiōnem eius — vidē lēctiōnem sequentem.

Atticum ipsum est ūna dialectus inter multās; Homērus praecēdit standardizātiōnem Atticam per saecula.

VitiumIgnōrāre positiōnem prīmam “μῆνιν” ut accidentālem, sine sīgnificātiōne compositiōnāle.

RectiusRecognōscere positiōnem inceptīvam ipsīus themae tōtīus operis ut āctum poēticum dēlīberātum maximae ēmphasis (Gradū VI, ōrdō=focus).

Prīmum verbum epicī maximē īnfluentis tōtīus litterātūrae Eurōpaeae est “īra” — chōsa nōn accidentālis, sed programmatica.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Ilias I.1-2: “μῆνιν ἄειδε θεὰ Πηληϊάδεω Ἀχιλῆος / οὐλομένην, ἣ μυρί᾽ Ἀχαιοῖς ἄλγε᾽ ἔθηκε” — versūs maximē citātī litterātūrae Graecae.
  • “Μῆνιν” (īra), verbum prīmum, est thema organizāns tōtīus Iliadis — positiō ēmphatica dēlīberāta.
  • Prīmae fōrmae dialectī Epicae/Iōnicae vīsae: genetīvī in -εω/-ος (Πηληϊάδεω, Ἀχιλῆος), praeparātiō prō lēctiōne sequentī.
  • Convention “in mediās rēs”: Ilias incipit ultimō annō bellī, nōn ab ovō — īnfluēns omnem epicum posteriōrem, inclūsō Vergiliō.
