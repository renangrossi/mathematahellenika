Gradus VII · Auctores

Lysias: Ōrātiōnēs Sēlectae

Verba authentica: initium ōrātiōnis “Prō Caede Eratosthenis” (1.1) — cum introductiōne ad genus “tenue” (ἰσχνὸν εἶδος) et ēthopoiiam.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Nota: discrīmine ā Xenophonte/Platōne (quōrum textūs supra hīc citātī sunt ex memoriā scholāsticā cōnfīdenter), Lysiae verba sunt hīc citāta MAGIS CONSERVĀTĪVĒ — ūna clausula initiālis, bene attestāta et commūniter anthologizāta in libris "introductiōnis ad ōrātōrēs Atticōs" — plūs commentāriō historicō/stilisticō dē Lysiā generāliter, prae extēnsā citātiōne verbōtenus quam nōn possum cōnfīdenter garantīre sine cōnsultātiōne ēditiōnis criticae.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Legere clausulam initiālem authenticam ōrātiōnis Lysiae 1 (“Prō Caede Eratosthenis”)
  • Situāre Lysiam historicē: logographus metoecus (nōn cīvis), scrīptor ōrātiōnum aliīs dēlīberandārum
  • Explicāre cōnceptum “ēthopoiiae” (Graecē ἠθοποιία): arte adaptandī vōcem ōrātiōnis ad persōnam locūtōris (nōn Lysiae ipsīus)
  • Recognōscere genetīvum pretiī (Gradū VI) et īnfīnītīvum articulārem in āctū authenticō

Explicatio

Explicatio

Lysias (c. 445–380 a.C.) erat metoecus (μέτοικος, resident aliēnigena sine cīvitāte plēnā) Athēnīs, quī post caedem frātris suī Polemarchī ā "Trīgintā Tyrannīs" (regimine oligarchicō breve post Bellum Peloponnēsiacum) prōfūgit, deinde reversus scrīpsit ōrātiōnēs FORENSĒS quās aliī cīvēs (nōn ipse Lysias, quī, ut metoecus, nōn poterat plēnē loquī prō sē in tribūnālī Atheniensī) memoriae mandābant et prōnūntiābant in iūdiciīs. Hic est logographus classicus — scrīptor prō aliīs.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

Textus: Lysias 1.1 (Initium, “Prō Caede Eratosthenis”)

Περὶ πολλοῦ ἂν ποιησαίμην, ὦ ἄνδρες...

Versiō Latīna: "Magnī facerem, ō virī..." (litt. "circā multum facerem").

Commentārius: περὶ πολλοῦ — GENETĪVUS PRETIĪ (Gradū VI!), "magnī, multī [valōris]". ἂν ποιησαίμην — OPTĀTĪVUS POTENTIĀLIS (Gradū V), "facerem, cōnsīderārem [sī...]" — modestia rhētorica classica, incohāns ōrātiōnem forensem cum tōnō temperātō, nōn assertīvō. Verbum ποιέω hīc medium, sēnsū "reputāre, aestimāre" (nōn sōlum "facere").

Ōrātiō integra ("Prō Caede Eratosthenis") narrat dēfēnsiōnem hominis accūsātī necāvisse mōechum uxōris suae, Eratosthenem — captum in flagrante — dēfendēns sē per lēgem Athēniēnsem quae permittēbat tāle occīdere. Genus "tenue" (ἰσχνόν) Lysiae — simplex, vērīsimile, sine ōrnāmentīs excessīvīs — famam eī comparāvit ut magister ēthopoiiae (ἠθοποιία): capācitāte reddendī vōcem ōrātiōnis convenientem persōnae/charactērī locūtōris (agricolae, mīlitis, senis...), nōn vōcem propriam Lysiae ipsīus.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • περὶ πολλοῦ ἂν ποιησαίμην — magnī facerem (genetīvus pretiī + optātīvus potentiālis)
  • ὦ ἄνδρες [δικασταί] — ō virī [iūdicēs]! (vocātīvus, fōrmula forensis standard)
  • μέτοικος — resident aliēnigena (status sociālis Lysiae Athēnīs)
  • λογογράφος — scrīptor ōrātiōnum prō aliīs (professiō Lysiae)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumCrēdere Lysiam ipsum prōnūntiāvisse ōrātiōnēs suās in tribūnālibus.

RectiusRecordārī Lysias, ut metoecus, scrīpsit ōrātiōnēs quās ALIĪ cīvēs (clientēs eius) memoriae mandābant et ipsī prōnūntiābant.

Distinctio inter auctōrem/scrīptōrem (Lysias) et locūtōrem/persōnam ōrātiōnis (client) est fundāmentālis prō intellēctū ēthopoiiae.

VitiumCōnfundere “genus tenue” (stilum simplicem Lysiae) cum “stilō īnferiōre” vel minus artificiōsō.

RectiusRecognōscere simplicitātem apparentem Lysiae ut ars altē refīnīta, dēlīberātē adaptāta ad vōcem “nātūrālem” locūtōris nōn-ōrātōriī.

Antīquī ipsī (Dionysius Halicarnassēnsis, alius rhētor) laudāvērunt Lysiam praecīsē propter hanc “simplicitātem artificiōsam”.

VitiumSuppōnere gradum eundem cōnfīdentiae verbōtenus prō Lysiā ac prō Xenophonte/Platōne/Homērō in hōc cursū.

RectiusRecognōscere explicitē (ut hoc ipsum caput indicat) quod citātiō hīc est magis conservātīva, ūna clausula bene attestāta, nōn extēnsa ōrātiō memoriter reproducta.

Honestās academica: nōn omnis auctor antīquus est aequāliter “memorābilis” verbōtenus sine cōnsultātiōne ēditiōnis; discere hoc discrīmen est pars māturitātis philologicae.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Lysias 1.1 (clausula initiālis, bene attestāta): “Περὶ πολλοῦ ἂν ποιησαίμην, ὦ ἄνδρες...” — genetīvus pretiī + optātīvus potentiālis.
  • Lysias erat metoecus Athēnīs, logographus (scrīptor ōrātiōnum prō aliīs cīvibus), post caedem frātris ā Trīgintā Tyrannīs.
  • Ēthopoiia: ars adaptandī vōcem ōrātiōnis ad persōnam/charactērem locūtōris, nōn vōcem propriam auctōris.
  • Citātiō hīc est dēlīberātē magis conservātīva quam Xenophōn/Platōn priōrēs — honestās academica dē līmitibus memoriae verbōtenus.
