Rhētorica Attica

Figūrae classicae: antithesis (μέν/δέ), tricōlon, asyndeton, chiasmus, anaphora, figūrae Gorgianae (parisōsis, homoeoteleuton) — īnstrūmenta ōrātōrum et historicōrum.

Prooemium

Rhētorica (ῥητορική) fuit disciplīna centrālis ēducātiōnis Atticae altiōris, ars persuādendī per structūram et ōrnātum verbōrum — nōn sōlum contentum logicum. Figūrae hīc collēctae nōn sunt “decorātiō” superficiālis, sed īnstrūmenta persuāsiōnis effectīva, adhibita ā philosophīs (Platōne), historicīs (Thucydidē), et ōrātōribus (Dēmosthene, Lysiā) aequāliter.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Recitāre et reconhōscere sex figūrās rhētoricās classicās frequentēs in prōsā Atticā artificiōsā
  • Applicāre μέν/δέ (Gradū VI) ut exemplum antithesis systēmaticae, iam nōtum
  • Reconhōscere “figūrās Gorgianas” (parisōsis, homoeoteleuton) ut īnfluentiam Gorgiae Leontīnī super prōsam Atticam artificiōsam
  • Analyzāre exemplum authenticum (via Plūtarchum) tricōlī cum asyndetō, phrasim maximē memorābilem in tōtā historiā Occidentālī

Explicatio

Explicatio

Multae figūrae hīc discendae sunt applicātiōnēs specializātae grammaticae iam nōtae (μέν/δέ, ōrdō verbōrum, particulae — omnia Gradū VI) — nōn theoria omnīnō nova, sed reconhōscere PATTERN rhētoricum ubi anteā vīdistī sōlum grammaticam "nūdam".

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Antithesis, Tricōlon, Asyndeton

Antithesis (ἀντίθεσις): oppositiō balanceāta duārum idēārum, systēmatice construēnda per μέν...δέ (iam nōtum, Gradū VI): ὁ μὲν λέγει, ὁ δὲ σιγᾷ. "Hic quidem loquitur, ille autem tacet." Tricōlon (τρίκωλον): sēriēs trium elementōrum parallēlōrum, frequenter crēscēns longitūdine ("tricōlon crescendō"). Asyndeton (ἀσύνδετον, "sine coniūnctiōne"): OMISSIO dēlīberāta particulārum connective (καί, δέ) inter elementa parallēla, creāns effectum rapidum/ēmphaticum — contrā convention normālem Graecam (Gradū VI) quae praeferit particulās explicitās. Exemplum classicum tricōlī CUM asyndetō (relātum ā Plūtarchō, historicō Graecō posteriōre, dē Caesare Rōmānō): ἦλθον, εἶδον, ἐνίκησα. "Vēnī, vīdī, vīcī" — trēs verba, sine ūllā coniūnctiōne, crēscentia in intēnsitāte dramaticā.

b) Chiasmus et Anaphora

Chiasmus (χιασμός, ā litterā χ, "chī", cūius fōrma X illūstrat structūram): ōrdō ABBA, ubi secunda medietas sententiae inversē reflectit prīmam: "nōn ut edam vīvō, sed ut vīvam edō" (patrōnus, exemplum illūstrātīvum). Anaphora (ἀναφορά, "referēns retrō"): repetītiō eiusdem verbī initiō clausulārum successīvārum, creāns rhythmum et ēmphasim accumulātīvam — frequēns apud Dēmosthenem in passibus ēmphaticīs.

c) Figūrae Gorgianae

Gorgias Leontīnus (sophista Siciliēnsis, saec. V a.C.) intrōdūxit Athēnās stilum "artificiōsum" prōsae, notābilem per: parisōsis (παρίσωσις, clausulae aequālis longitūdinis, structūrā balanceātae) et homoeoteleuton (ὁμοιοτέλευτον, "similis fīnis", clausulae fīnientēs sonō similī, comparābile rhythmō, nōn rīmae poēticae stricte). Hae figūrae, quamvīs interdum crīticātae ut excessīvae (ā Platōne ipsō, quī parodiat stilum Gorgianum in dialogīs suīs), īnfluxērunt prōfundē ōrātōrēs Atticōs posteriōrēs.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • ὁ μὲν λέγει, ὁ δὲ σιγᾷ. — Hic loquitur, ille tacet. (antithesis, μέν/δέ)
  • ἦλθον, εἶδον, ἐνίκησα. — Vēnī, vīdī, vīcī. (tricōlon crescendō + asyndeton, via Plūtarchum)
  • μὴ γένοιτο! (Gradū VII priōre) — exemplum brevitātis ēmphaticae, comparābile effectuī asyndetī
  • ἀνεξέταστος βίος οὐ βιωτὸς ἀνθρώπῳ. (Platōn, Gradū VII priōre) — exemplum concīsitātis aphoristicae, alia figūra rhētorica (sententia/γνώμη)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumTractāre figūrās rhētoricās ut “decorātiōnem” superficiālem sine fūnctiōne argūmentātīvā reālī.

RectiusRecognōscere figūrās ut īnstrūmenta persuāsiōnis effectīva: antithesis clārificat oppositiōnem, tricōlon crescendō intēnsificat ēmphasim, asyndeton accelerat rhythmum.

Ōrātōrēs antīquī (et modernī) nōn adhibent hās figūrās gratuītē — quaeque servit obiectīvō persuāsīvō praecīsō.

VitiumCrēdere asyndeton esse error grammaticus (omissiō particulae obligātōriae), quia Gradus VI docuit particulās esse essentiālēs.

RectiusRecognōscere asyndeton ut violātiōnem DĒLĪBERĀTAM conventiōnis normālis (particulae explicitae), praecīsē prō effectū rhētoricō intentō.

Sīcut hyperbaton (Gradū VI), asyndeton est figūra intentiōnālis, nōn error — discipulus dēbet distinguere “violātiōnem rhētoricam dēlīberātam” ab “errōre grammaticō genuīnō”.

VitiumSuppōnere hās figūrās esse exclūsīvē Graecās, sine relātiōne ad trāditiōnem rhētoricam Occidentālem posteriōrem (Latīnam, et linguās modernās).

RectiusRecognōscere hās figūrās (antithesis, tricōlon, chiasmus, anaphora) ut fundāmentum tōtīus trāditiōnis rhētoricae Occidentālis, adhūc adhibitum ab ōrātōribus/scrīptōribus modernīs.

Rhētorica Graeca (systēmatizāta ab Aristotele) fuit trānsmissa per Rōmānōs (Cicerō, Quīntiliānus) usque ad ēducātiōnem modernam.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Antithesis: oppositiō balanceāta, typicē μέν/δέ (Gradū VI). Tricōlon: sēriēs trium elementōrum, saepe crēscēns.
  • Asyndeton: omissiō dēlīberāta particulārum connectivārum, prō effectū rapidō/ēmphaticō — violātiō intentiōnālis, nōn error.
  • Chiasmus (ABBA) et anaphora (repetītiō initiālis): figūrae additiōnālēs structūrae/rhythmī.
  • Figūrae Gorgianae (parisōsis, homoeoteleuton): stilus artificiōsus intrōductus ā Gorgiā, īnfluēns prōsam Atticam posteriōrem.
  • Exemplum classicum (via Plūtarchum): “ἦλθον, εἶδον, ἐνίκησα” — tricōlon crescendō cum asyndetō, phrasis maximē memorābilis.
