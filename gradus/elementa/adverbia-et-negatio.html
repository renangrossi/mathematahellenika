Gradus II · Elementa

Adverbia et Negātiō

Fōrmātiō adverbiōrum ab adiectīvīs (-ως), et discrīmen fundāmentāle inter negātiōnēs οὐ et μή.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Ὁ διδάσκαλος καλῶς λέγει. Ἀγάθων λέγει· οὐκ ἐθέλω μένειν. Notāvistī: καλῶς (adverbium, “bene”) et οὐκ (negātiō)? Duo elementa parva sed frequentissima concludunt hunc gradum grammaticum praecipuum: quōmodo adverbia fōrmantur ab adiectīvīs iam nōtīs, et quōmodo Graecum ēligit inter οὐ et μή secundum grammaticam contextūs.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Formāre adverbium ab adiectīvō classis prīmae, mūtandō -ν fīnāle genetīvī plūrālis in -ς
  • Recitāre trēs fōrmās negātiōnis οὐ (οὐ, οὐκ, οὐχ) et applicāre rēgulam orthographicam correctam
  • Distinguere ūsum fundāmentālem οὐ (negātiō factī, modō indicātīvō) ā μή (negātiō voluntātis/hypothesis, aliīs modīs)
  • Applicāre μή ad prohibitiōnēs simplicēs cum imperātīvō

Explicatio

Explicatio

Ὁ διδάσκαλος καλῶς λέγει· δικαίως καὶ σοφῶς ἄρχει. Ἀγάθων λέγει· “Οὐκ ἐθέλω μένειν.” Μελίτη λέγει· “Οὐχ ὁρῶ τὴν πόλιν.” Ὁ διδάσκαλος λέγει· “Μὴ λύε τὸν δοῦλον.” (Magister bene loquitur; iūstē et sapienter regit. Agathōn dīcit: “Nōlō manēre.” Melitē dīcit: “Nōn videō urbem.” Magister dīcit: “Nōlī solvere servum.”)

Notāvistīne καλῶς, δικαίως, σοφῶς (adverbia in -ως) versus οὐκ, οὐχ, μή (trēs negātiōnēs dīversae)? Adverbium fōrmātur ab adiectīvō classis prīmae rēgulā simplicī: sūme genetīvum plūrālem (masculīnum/neutrum, quī semper fīnītur -ων), et mūta -ν fīnāle in -ς: καλός → gen. pl. καλῶν → adverbium καλῶς (“bene, pulchrē”). Discrīmen quod Latīnum nōn habet: Latīnum adhibet “nōn” ubīque; Graecum ēligit inter οὐ et μή secundum grammaticam contextūs — vidēbis rēgulās plēnās īnfrā.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Fōrmātiō Adverbiōrum

Rēgula valet prō omnī adiectīvō classis prīmae, indepēndenter ā fōrmā fēminīnī (-η vs. -α) — sōlum genetīvus plūrālis masculīnus/neutrus (semper -ων) importat.

b) Οὐ, Οὐκ, Οὐχ: Rēgula Orthographica

Οὐ mūtat fōrmam secundum sonum initiālem verbī sequentis, exāctē sīcut articulus Anglicus “a”/“an”: οὐ ante cōnsonantem (οὐ λύω), οὐκ ante vōcālem cum spīritū lēnī (οὐκ ἐθέλω), οὐχ ante vōcālem cum spīritū aspero (οὐχ ὁρῶ). Mūtātiō sequitur logicam eufōnicam: -κ (surdum) ante spīritum lēnem, -χ (aspīrātum, comparā θ/φ/χ ex Gradū I) ante spīritum asperum, quia aspīrātiō “concordat”.

c) Οὐ vs. Μή: Discrīmen Fundāmentāle

Οὐ negat FACTA obiectīva, modō indicātīvō: οὐ λύω τὸν δοῦλον “nōn solvō servum” (āctiō rēalis, negāta). Μή negat voluntātem, hypothesim, commandum — omnia quae NŌN sunt affirmātiōnēs simplicēs dē factō: imperātīva (prohibitiō), optātīva, coniūnctīva, condiciōnēs, plēraeque īnfīnītīvī. Exemplum ūtile iam nunc: prohibitiō cum imperātīvō semper adhibet μή, numquam οὐ: μὴ λύε “nōlī solvere!”. Theoria plēna huius discrīminis (per omnēs modōs) sequitur cum coniūnctīvus/optātīvus/imperātīvus plēnē tractantur (Gradibus IV/V).

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • ὁ διδάσκαλος καλῶς λέγει. — Magister bene loquitur. (adverbium ab adiectīvō)
  • οὐ λύω τὸν δοῦλον. — Nōn solvō servum. (negātiō factī, indicātīvus)
  • οὐκ ἐθέλω μένειν. — Nōlō manēre. (οὐκ ante spīritum lēnem)
  • οὐχ ὁρῶ τὴν πόλιν. — Nōn videō urbem. (οὐχ ante spīritum asperum)
  • μὴ λύε τὸν δοῦλον. — Nōlī solvere servum! (prohibitiō, imperātīvus + μή)
  • δικαίως καὶ σοφῶς ἄρχει. — Iūstē et sapienter regit. (duo adverbia coniūncta)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumAdhibēre οὐ ubīque, indepēndenter ā modō vel cōnstrūctiōne, imitandō ūnicam negātiōnem Latīnam “nōn”.

RectiusDistinguere: οὐ prō factīs (indicātīvus), μή prō voluntāte/hypothesī/commandō (aliī modī, imperātīvus, plērīque īnfīnītīvī).

Latīnum habet ūnam sōlam negātiōnem prīncipālem (nōn); Graecum distinguit systēmaticē duo genera negātiōnis.

VitiumIgnōrāre mūtātiōnem οὐ → οὐκ/οὐχ ante vōcālēs, scrībendō semper “οὐ” invariābiliter.

RectiusApplicāre rēgulam eufōnicam: οὐκ ante spīritum lēnem, οὐχ ante spīritum asperum, οὐ ante cōnsonantem.

Error orthographicus frequēns, comparābilis neglēctuī rēgulae ν ephelkystikon (Gradū I).

VitiumFōrmāre adverbium ex fōrmā fēminīnā adiectīvī (ut ex καλή) potius quam ex genetīvō plūrālī masculīnō/neutrō.

RectiusSemper fōrmāre adverbium ā genetīvō plūrālī masculīnō/neutrō (-ων → -ως), indepēndenter ā fōrmā fēminīnī.

Fōrma fēminīna nōn participat in fōrmātiōne adverbiī — sōlum genus masculīnum/neutrum importat hīc.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Adverbium: gen. pl. masc./neut. adiectīvī (-ων) → adverbium (-ως); καλός → καλῶν → καλῶς.
  • Οὐ (ante cōnsonantem), οὐκ (ante spīritum lēnem), οὐχ (ante spīritum asperum) — mūtātiō eufōnica obligātōria.
  • Οὐ negat facta obiectīva (indicātīvus); μή negat voluntātem/hypothesim/commandum (aliī modī).
  • Prohibitiō cum imperātīvō semper adhibet μή, numquam οὐ (μὴ λύε “nōlī solvere”).
  • Theoria plēna οὐ/μή per omnēs modōs sequitur cum coniūnctīvus/optātīvus/imperātīvus plēnē tractantur.
