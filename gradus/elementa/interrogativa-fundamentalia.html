Gradus II · Elementa

Interrogātīva Fundāmentālia

Prōnōmen interrogātīvum τίς/τί (dēclīnātum) et adverbia interrogātīva commūnia (ποῦ, πότε, πῶς, διὰ τί).

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Μελίτη λέγει· τίς εἶ; Ἀγάθων λέγει· Ἀγάθων εἰμί. Σὺ δὲ τίς εἶ; Notāvistī hoc iam ex fābulā prīmā. Nunc τίς/τί dēclīnātur plēnē, et alia verba interrogātīva (ποῦ, πόθεν...) sē adiungunt.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Dēclīnāre prōnōmen interrogātīvum τίς (masc./fem.) et τί (neutrum) per omnēs cāsūs, utrōque numerō
  • Distinguere τίς interrogātīvum (accentū semper acūtō, numquam gravī) ā τις indēfīnītō (enclīticō, sēnsū “aliquis”)
  • Recitāre adverbia interrogātīva commūnia: ποῦ, ποῖ, πόθεν, πότε, πῶς, διὰ τί
  • Formāre quaestiōnēs simplicēs cum hīs verbīs interrogātīvīs

Explicatio

Explicatio

Μελίτη λέγει· “Τίς εἶ;” Ἀγάθων λέγει· “Ἀγάθων εἰμί. Σὺ δὲ τίς εἶ;” Μελίτη λέγει· “Μελίτη εἰμί.” “Πόθεν ἥκεις;” “Ἐκ τῆς πόλεως. Καὶ σύ, ποῦ μένεις;” “Ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ τοῦ διδασκάλου. Τίνος ἐστὶ τὸ βιβλίον;” (Melitē dīcit: “Quis es?” Agathōn dīcit: “Agathōn sum. Tū vērō quis es?” Melitē dīcit: “Melitē sum.” “Unde venīs?” “Ex urbe. Et tū, ubi manēs?” “In domō magistrī. Cuius est liber?”)

Notāvistīne τίς, τίνος, πόθεν, ποῦ — quattuor verba interrogātīva dīversa? Τίς (masc./fem.) et τί (neutrum) significant “quis?/quae?/quod?” cum sōlum vōcālem, vel “quī? quālis?” cum adiectīvō (τίς ἀνήρ; “quī vir?”). Fōrma haec est PRŌNŌMEN interrogātīvum — nōn cōnfundenda cum prōnōmine indēfīnītō τις/τι (“aliquis, quīdam”), quod habet fōrmās scrīptūrā identicās sed est enclīticum (sine accentū proprio, ex Gradū I).

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Τίς/Τί: Dēclīnātiō Plēna

Nōm.τίςτίτίνεςτίνα
Gen.τίνοςτίνοςτίνωντίνων

Nōtā (rēgula aurea neutrōrum, ex Gradū II priōre): nōm. = acc. neutrī, singulārī et plūrālī. Nōtā etiam thema commūne τίν- vīsibile in plūribus cāsibus — indicium formae dēclīnātiōnis tertiae quam mox vidēbis systēmatice.

b) Τίς Interrogātīvum vs. Τις Indēfīnītum

Duo prōnōmina scrībuntur IDENTIDEM (τις/τι in fōrmā basicā) sed differunt ACCENTŪ: τίς (interrogātīvum, “quis?”) habet SEMPER accentum acūtum, quī numquam mūtātur in gravem, etiam ante aliud verbum sine pausā (exceptiō ūnica ad rēgulam gravis, ex Gradū I) — hoc ipsum accentus persistēns est signum vīsuāle rapidum quō interrogātīvum ā cēterīs verbīs distinguitur. τις (indēfīnītum, “aliquis, quīdam”) est enclīticum (sine accentū proprio, ex Gradū I), sequēns verbum praecēdēns: ἄνθρωπός τις “homō quīdam”.

Nōtā trichotomiam ποῦ/ποῖ/πόθεν, exāctē parallēla trichotomiae ἐν/εἰς/ἐκ (lēctiō priōre): status/mōtus-versus/orīgō — patrōnus recurrēns per tōtam syntaxem Graecam locālem.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • τίς εἶ; — Quis es? (iam nōtum ex Gradū I)
  • τί λέγεις; — Quid dīcis? (neutrum, obiectum)
  • τίνος ἐστὶ τὸ βιβλίον; — Cuius est liber? (genetīvus possessōris)
  • ποῦ μένεις; — Ubi manēs?
  • πόθεν ἥκεις; — Unde venīs?
  • διὰ τί οὐ λέγεις; — Cūr nōn dīcis? (interrogātiō + negātiō)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumCōnfundere τίς (interrogātīvum, accentū persistente) cum τις (indēfīnītum, enclīticum) propter fōrmam scrīptūrae identicam.

RectiusDistinguere per accentum: τίς semper habet accentum acūtum vīsibilem; τις enclīticum caret accentū proprio.

Discrīmen sēnsūs est fundāmentāle: “quis vēnit?” (τίς ἦλθεν;) vs. “aliquis vēnit” (ἦλθέ τις).

VitiumApplicāre rēgulam normālem gravis (acūtus → gravis ante aliud verbum) ad τίς interrogātīvum.

RectiusRecordārī τίς interrogātīvum est exceptiō: accentus manet semper acūtus, numquam mūtātur in gravem.

Haec exceptiō ipsa est ūtilis signum: sī vidēs τίς cum accentū acūtō persistente ante aliud verbum, scīs statim esse interrogātīvum.

VitiumNōn distinguere ποῦ/ποῖ/πόθεν, adhibendō ūnam prō omnibus tribus sēnsibus (status/mōtus/orīgō).

RectiusApplicāre eandem logicam ac ἐν/εἰς/ἐκ: ποῦ=ubi (status), ποῖ=quō (mōtus), πόθεν=unde (orīgō).

Trichotomia locālis recurrit systēmaticē per Graecum; recognōscere patrōnum semel adiuvat memoriam omnium trium sēriērum.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Τίς/τί (interrogātīvum): nōm. τίς/τί, gen. τίνος, dat. τίνι, acc. τίνα/τί (sg.) — τίνες/τίνα, τίνων, τίσι(ν), τίνας/τίνα (pl.).
  • Τίς interrogātīvum retinet SEMPER accentum acūtum (exceptiō ad rēgulam gravis); τις indēfīnītum est enclīticum.
  • Adverbia interrogātīva: ποῦ (ubi), ποῖ (quō), πόθεν (unde), πότε (quandō), πῶς (quōmodo), διὰ τί (cūr).
  • Trichotomia ποῦ/ποῖ/πόθεν (status/mōtus/orīgō) parallēla praepositiōnibus ἐν/εἰς/ἐκ.
