Gradus II · Elementa

Praepositiōnēs Fundāmentālēs

Praepositiōnēs cum cāsū fīxō (ἐν, εἰς, ἐκ, ἀπό, σύν, πρός) et praepositiōnēs cum sēnsū variante secundum cāsum (διά, μετά, ὑπό).

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Ἀγάθων καὶ Μελίτη ἐν τῇ πόλει μένουσιν. Βαίνουσι σὺν τοῖς φίλοις πρὸς τὴν ἀγοράν. Notāvistī: ἐν τῇ πόλει (status) vs. πρὸς τὴν ἀγοράν (mōtus) — eadem “in” Latīnē, sed cāsus dīversus? Praepositiō Graeca — sīcut Latīna — regit cāsum nōminis sequentis; sed discrīmen praecipuum ā Latīnō est: multae praepositiōnēs Graecae mūtant sēnsum secundum cāsum quem regunt.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Recitāre sex praepositiōnēs commūnēs cum cāsū fīxō ūnicō (ἐν+dat., εἰς+acc., ἐκ+gen., ἀπό+gen., σύν+dat., πρός+acc.)
  • Explicāre logicam generālem: genetīvus post praepositiōnem saepe significat “ā/ex”, datīvus significat locum statūrium, accūsātīvus significat mōtum versus
  • Distinguere sēnsūs dīversōs eiusdem praepositiōnis secundum cāsum sequentem (διά, μετά, ὑπό)
  • Trānsferre phrasēs praepositiōnālēs simplicēs correcte, attendendō semper cāsum

Explicatio

Explicatio

Ἀγάθων καὶ Μελίτη ἐν τῇ πόλει μένουσιν. Βαίνουσι σὺν τοῖς φίλοις πρὸς τὴν ἀγοράν, ἐκ τῆς οἰκίας. Μετὰ τὴν ὁδόν, πάλιν εἰς τὴν οἰκίαν βαίνουσιν. (Agathōn et Melitē in urbe manent. Eunt cum amīcīs ad forum, ē domō. Post iter, rūrsus in domum eunt.)

Notāvistīne ἐν τῇ πόλει (dat., status), πρὸς τὴν ἀγοράν (acc., mōtus versus), ἐκ τῆς οἰκίας (gen., orīgō), εἰς τὴν οἰκίαν (acc., mōtus versus)? Logica generālis (ūtilis ut mnēmonicum, nōn rēgula absolūta): genetīvus post praepositiōnem saepe indicat origine/sēparātiōnem (“ā/ex”, unde vēnit āctiō); datīvus indicat locum statūrium vel īnstrūmentum (“in, cum”, ubi vel quō īnstrūmentō āctiō fit); accūsātīvus indicat mōtum versus vel extēnsiōnem (“ad, per”, quō āctiō tendit). Haec logica nōn explicat omnem cāsum omnis praepositiōnis, sed adiuvat memoriam.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Praepositiōnēs cum Cāsū Fīxō

PraepositiōCāsusSēnsusExemplum
ἐνDat.in, apud (locus statūrius)ἐν τῇ πόλει
εἰςAcc.in, ad (mōtus versus)εἰς τὴν πόλιν
ἐκ (ἐξ)Gen.ē, ex (mōtus ā)ἐκ τῆς πόλεως
ἀπόGen.ā, ab (sēparātiō)ἀπὸ τῆς οἰκίας
σύνDat.cum (comitātus)σὺν τοῖς φίλοις
πρόςAcc.ad, versusπρὸς τὴν πόλιν

Nōtā ἐν vs. εἰς: ambae trānsferuntur saepe “in” Latīnē, sed ἐν (+ dat.) est locus STATUS (“in urbe [iam]”), εἰς (+ acc.) est mōtus VERSUS (“in urbem [euntem]”) — discrīmen exāctē analogum discrīminī Latīnō “in” + ablātīvō vs. “in” + accūsātīvō!

b) Praepositiōnēs cum Sēnsū Variante: διά, μετά

Praepositiō+ Genetīvus+ Accūsātīvus
διάper (spatium/tempus): διὰ τῆς πόλεως “per urbem”propter, ob (causa): διὰ τὸν φόβον “propter timōrem”
μετάcum (comitātus): μετὰ τῶν φίλων “cum amīcīs”post (tempus/locum): μετὰ τὴν μάχην “post pugnam”

c) Ὑπό: Trēs Cāsūs, Trēs Sēnsūs

ὑπό + genetīvus = “ab” (agēns passīvus, iam vīsum in lēctiōne priōre): λύεται ὑπὸ τοῦ δεσπότου “solvitur ā dominō”. ὑπό + datīvus = “sub” (locus statūrius, rārior in prōsā, frequentior in poēsī): ὑπὸ τῷ δένδρῳ “sub arbore”. ὑπό + accūsātīvus = “sub” (mōtus versus): ὑπὸ τὸ δένδρον “sub arborem [euntem]”. Eadem logica ac ἐν/εἰς: datīvus = status, accūsātīvus = mōtus.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • οἱ πολῖται ἐν τῇ πόλει μένουσιν. — Cīvēs in urbe manent. (ἐν + dat., status)
  • οἱ στρατιῶται εἰς τὴν πόλιν βαίνουσιν. — Mīlitēs in urbem eunt. (εἰς + acc., mōtus)
  • ἥκομεν ἐκ τῆς οἰκίας. — Venimus ē domō. (ἐκ + gen., orīgō)
  • διὰ τὸν πόλεμον φεύγομεν. — Propter bellum fugimus. (διά + acc., causa)
  • μετὰ τὴν μάχην ἐπανερχόμεθα. — Post pugnam revertimur. (μετά + acc., tempus)
  • βαίνει σὺν τοῖς φίλοις πρὸς τὴν ἀγοράν. — It cum amīcīs ad forum. (σύν + dat.; πρός + acc.)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumCōnfundere ἐν (+ dat., status) cum εἰς (+ acc., mōtus), adhibendō ūnam prō alterā indiscrīminātim.

RectiusAttendere semper: ἐν indicat “ubi [iam]”, εἰς indicat “quō [euntem]” — discrīmen praecīsē analogum Latīnō in+abl. vs. in+acc.

Discrīmen status/mōtūs est fundāmentāle in syntaxī Graecā praepositiōnum, saepe neglēctum ab incipientibus.

VitiumTrānsferre διά vel μετά semper eōdem modō, sine attendere cāsum sequentem.

RectiusVidēre cāsum PRIMUM, deinde ēligere sēnsum correctum: διά+gen. “per”, διά+acc. “propter”; μετά+gen. “cum”, μετά+acc. “post”.

Errāre hīc mūtat sēnsum tōtīus sententiae, nōn sōlum stylum — “per urbem” ≠ “propter urbem”.

VitiumCrēdere ὑπό significāre sōlum “sub”, ignōrandō sēnsum “ab” (agēns passīvus) cum genetīvō.

RectiusRecordārī ὑπό + genetīvus in cōnstrūctiōne passīvā semper significat “ab”, nōn “sub”.

Sēnsus “ab” (agēns) est ūsus frequentissimus ipsīus ὑπό in prōsā classicā, magis quam sēnsus locālis “sub”.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Cāsū fīxō: ἐν+dat. (in, status), εἰς+acc. (in, mōtus), ἐκ+gen. (ē/ex), ἀπό+gen. (ā/ab), σύν+dat. (cum), πρός+acc. (ad).
  • Logica generālis: genetīvus = orīgō/sēparātiō, datīvus = status, accūsātīvus = mōtus versus.
  • Διά: +gen. “per” (spatium), +acc. “propter” (causa). Μετά: +gen. “cum”, +acc. “post”.
  • Ὑπό: +gen. “ab” (agēns passīvus), +dat./+acc. “sub” (status/mōtus).
  • Attendere semper cāsum post praepositiōnem: sēnsus tōtīus phrāsis inde pendet, nōn sōlum grammatica.
