Gradus IV · Media

Aoristus Prīmus: Āctīvus et Medius

Aoristus “prīmus” (sigmaticus, regulāris): λύω → ἔλυσα; sēnsus fundāmentālis aoristī (āctiō simplex, singulāris, sine referentiā ad continuitātem).

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Praeteritum Graecum nōn est ūnum sōlum tempus (dīversē ā Latīnō, ubi perfectum servit ambōbus sēnsibus): imperfectum (Gradū III, āctiō continua) et aoristus (hodiē, āctiō simplex/pūnctuālis) sunt DUO tempora praeteritī distīncta, cum sēnsibus aspectuālibus dīversīs, nōn temporālibus. “Aoristus” (Graecē ἀόριστος, “illīmitātus/indefinitus”) significat āctiōnem vīsam ut ēventum simplicem, sine attentiōne ad ducātiōnem vel repetitiōnem.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Coniugāre plēnē aoristum āctīvum et medium “prīmum” (sigmaticum) verbī λύω
  • Explicāre sēnsum aspectuālem aoristī: āctiō vīsa ut PŪNCTUM simplex, opposita imperfectō (āctiō continua)
  • Formāre īnfīnītīvum aoristī āctīvī (λῦσαι) et medium (λύσασθαι), notandō accentum speciālem īnfīnītīvī āctīvī
  • Distinguere augmentum + thema + σα (aoristus) ā augmentō + thema (imperfectum, sine σα)

Explicatio

Explicatio

Discrīmen aoristī ab imperfectō nōn est QUANDO āctiō facta est, sed QUŌMODO loquēns eam videt: ἔλυον (imperfectum) = “solvēbam” (continuē, per tempus); ἔλυσα (aoristus) = “solvī” (semel, ut ēventus simplex). Discrīmen aspectuāle, nōn temporāle stricte — comparā discrīmen imperfectō/perfectō simplicī Lūsitānicō (“eu soltava” vs. “eu soltei”).

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Aoristus Āctīvus: ἔλυσα

PersōnaSingulārisPlūrālis
1ªἔλυσαἐλύσαμεν
2ªἔλυσαςἐλύσατε
3ªἔλυσε(ν)ἔλυσαν

Fōrmātiō: augmentum + thema + σα (signum aoristī “prīmī”) + dēsinentia. Compara cum imperfectō (ἔλυον, sine σα): differentia vīsibilis est praecīsē hoc σα īnsertum. Īnfīnītīvus: λῦσαι — nōtā accentum recessīvum speciālem in ultimā (paenultimā hīc, quia -αι fīnāle est “brevis” prō rēgulīs accentūs, exceptiō notābilis).

b) Aoristus Medius: ἐλυσάμην

PersōnaSingulārisPlūrālis
1ªἐλυσάμηνἐλυσάμεθα
2ªἐλύσωἐλύσασθε
3ªἐλύσατοἐλύσαντο

Nōtā ἐλύσω (2ª sg.): fōrma historica *ἐλύσασο contracta (σ intervōcālicum periit, ασο→ω) — patrōnus iam nōtus ex praesentī/futūrō mediō (Gradibus II/III). Īnfīnītīvus medius: λύσασθαι.

c) “Prīmus” vs. “Secundus”: Praevīsiō

Hic aoristus vocātur “prīmus” (vel “sigmaticus”, “dēbilis”) quia adhibet σ ut signum characteristicum, applicābile ad maiōrem partem verbōrum. Multa verba frequentissima (praesertim brevia, thema monosyllabicum) adhibent in locō aoristum “secundum” (vel “fortem”), sine σ, cum thema ipsō mūtātō — patrōnus quem proximā lēctiōne vidēbis. Ambō genera significant idem aspectum (āctiō simplex); differunt sōlum morphologicē, nōn semanticē.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • ἔλυσα τὸν δοῦλον χθές. — Solvī servum herī. (āctiō simplex, ūnō mōmentō)
  • ἐλύσαμεν τὴν πόλιν ἐκ τῶν πολεμίων. — Līberāvimus urbem ab hostibus.
  • ἐλύσω σεαυτόν; — Līberāvistīne tē ipsum? (medium reflexīvum, 2ª sg.)
  • βουλόμεθα λῦσαι τὸν νόμον. — Volumus solvere/abrogāre lēgem. (īnfīnītīvus)
  • οἱ στρατιῶται ἔλυσαν τὰς πύλας. — Mīlitēs solvērunt portās.
  • τί ἐποίησας; — Quid fēcistī? (aoristus verbī contractī, augmentum + contractiō + σα)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumCōnfundere sēnsum aoristī cum sēnsū imperfectī, crēdēns ambō significāre sōlum “praeteritum” sine discrīmine aspectūs.

RectiusDistinguere: imperfectum = āctiō continua/repetīta; aoristus = āctiō simplex, vīsa ut pūnctum.

Discrīmen aspectuāle est fundāmentāle prō intellēctū prōsae Graecae; error hīc distorquet sēnsum nārrātīvum.

VitiumOmittere σ characteristicum, cōnfundendō aoristum āctīvum prīmum cum imperfectō (ἔλυον vs. ἔλυσα).

RectiusAttendere σα (vel σ sōlum ante vōcālem) ut signum distīnctīvum morphologicum aoristī prīmī.

Sine hōc signō, discipulus nōn potest distinguere tempora morphologicē, sōlum contextuāliter (minus fīdēle).

VitiumApplicāre eundem sēnsum ad omne verbum cuius aoristus est “secundus” (sine σ), crēdēns eōs esse aspectuāliter dīversōs ab aoristō prīmō.

RectiusRecordārī aoristus prīmus et secundus sunt purē morphologicē dīversī; sēnsus aspectuālis (āctiō simplex) est identicus in ambōbus.

Terminologia “prīmus/secundus” refert ad classem morphologicam, nōn ad differentiam significātiōnis.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Aoristus āctīvus λύω: ἔλυσα, ἔλυσας, ἔλυσε(ν) // ἐλύσαμεν, ἐλύσατε, ἔλυσαν. Īnfīnītīvus: λῦσαι.
  • Aoristus medius: ἐλυσάμην, ἐλύσω, ἐλύσατο // ἐλυσάμεθα, ἐλύσασθε, ἐλύσαντο. Īnfīnītīvus: λύσασθαι.
  • Aoristus vidēt āctiōnem ut PŪNCTUM simplex (aspectus), opposita imperfectō (āctiō continua) — discrīmen aspectuāle, nōn tantum temporāle.
  • Aoristus “prīmus” (sigmaticus, σα) est patrōnus regulāris; aoristus “secundus” (proxima lēctiō) est alius patrōnus morphologicus prō eōdem aspectū.
