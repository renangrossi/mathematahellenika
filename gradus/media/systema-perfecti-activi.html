Gradus IV · Media

Systēma Perfectī Āctīvī

Perfectum āctīvum (λέλυκα): reduplicātiō, et sēnsus proprius — status praesēns resultāns ex āctiōne praeteritā, dīversus ab aoristō.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Tertium et ultimum tempus praeteritī-related Graecum est perfectum, cum sēnsū prōrsus proprium: nōn “solvī” (aoristus, āctiō praeteritī simplex) neque “solvēbam” (imperfectum, āctiō continua), sed “solvī et adhūc status ille manet” — status praesēns, resultāns ex āctiōne iam factā. Graecum distinguit hoc systemāticē per fōrmam propriam, ubi Anglicum adhibet “have/has” + participium (“I have solved”), Lūsitānicum saepe locūtiōnem similem.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Coniugāre plēnē perfectum āctīvum indicātīvum verbī λύω (λέλυκα)
  • Applicāre rēgulam reduplicātiōnis: cōnsonāns initiālis + ε praefīxa (λύω → λε-λυκ-α)
  • Explicāre sēnsum proprium perfectī: status praesēns resultāns ex āctiōne praeteritā (“habeō id solūtum” magis quam “solvī”)
  • Distinguere sēnsum perfectī ab aoristō, comparandō cum discrīmine analogō in Anglicō (“I have solved” vs. “I solved”)

Explicatio

Explicatio

Perfectum indicat: āctiō est COMPLĒTA, et RESULTĀTUM eius persistit in praesentī. λέλυκα τὸν δοῦλον nōn sōlum “solvī servum [aliquandō]” (quod esset aoristus, ἔλυσα), sed “habeō servum solūtum [nunc]” — attentiō ad statum praesentem, nōn ad ēventum praeteritum ipsum.

Fōrmātiō: REDUPLICĀTIŌ (nōn augmentum!) + thema + κα + dēsinentiae speciālēs. Īnfīnītīvus: λελυκέναι.

b) Rēgula Reduplicātiōnis

Duae cōnsonantēs vel σ/ζ initiālēsε- sōlum (sīcut augmentum syllabicum)γράφω → γέγραφα (sed στρατεύω → ἐστράτευκα)

Reduplicātiō (nōn “augmentum”, terminus reservātus praeteritō) est signum characteristicum PROPRIUM perfectī, praesēns in OMNĪ fōrmā perfectī (indicātīvō, īnfīnītīvō, participiō) — dīversē ab augmentō, quod appāret sōlum in modō indicātīvō.

c) Perfectum vs. Aoristus: Discrīmen Semanticum

Comparā: ἔλυσα τὸν δοῦλον (aoristus) = “solvī servum” (ēventus praeteritus simplex, sine attentiōne ad statum praesentem — fortasse servus iterum captus est posteā). λέλυκα τὸν δοῦλον (perfectum) = “habeō servum solūtum” (status praesēns explicitus: servus ADHŪC est līber, nunc, dum loquor). Discrīmen est subtīlis sed reāle, et frequenter ūtile in argūmentātiōne rhētoricā/philosophicā Graecā (“probāvī” [aoristus, ēventus] vs. “dēmōnstrāvī et status manet vērus” [perfectum]).

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • λέλυκα τὸν δοῦλον· νῦν ἐλεύθερός ἐστιν. — Solvī servum; nunc līber est. (perfectum, status persistēns)
  • γέγραφα τὴν ἐπιστολήν. — Scrīpsī epistulam [et iam exstat, complēta]. (γράφω → γέγραφα)
  • πέπαυμαι — ita, sed hoc est medium; exemplum āctīvum melius: λελύκασι τὰς πύλας. — Solvērunt portās [et iam apertae manent].
  • οἶδα τὴν ἀλήθειαν. — Sciō vēritātem. (perfectum verbī εἴδω, sēnsū praesentī, forma valdē irregulāris et frequēns)
  • τί πέπραχας; — Quid fēcistī/ēgistī [et quō in statū nunc es propter id]? (πράττω → πέπραχα)
  • λελύκαμεν τὸν νόμον. — Abrogāvimus lēgem [et manet abrogāta].

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumCōnfundere reduplicātiōnem cum augmentō, crēdēns ambō esse idem phaenomenon morphologicum.

RectiusDistinguere: augmentum appāret sōlum in indicātīvō praeteritī; reduplicātiō appāret in OMNĪ fōrmā perfectī (indicātīvō, īnfīnītīvō, participiō).

Terminologia distīncta reflectit fūnctiōnem distīnctam: reduplicātiō est pars ipsīus themae perfectī, nōn signum modī indicātīvī.

VitiumTrānsferre perfectum Graecum exāctē sīcut aoristum (“solvī”), ignōrandō nuance status-praesentis.

RectiusTrānsferre perfectum cum attentiōne ad resultātum persistēns: “habeō solūtum” magis quam sōlum “solvī”.

Perdere hanc nuance impedit intellēctum plēnum argūmentātiōnis Graecae, ubi discrīmen perfectī/aoristī saepe est rhētoricē significāns.

VitiumCrēdere reduplicātiōnem esse semper simplex ε- praefīxum, sine cōnsīderāre variāns.

RectiusApplicāre rēgulam secundum initium verbī: cōnsonāns simplex → cōnsonāns+ε; vōcālis → allongātiō; duae cōnsonantēs/σ/ζ → ε- sōlum.

Variātiō reduplicātiōnis sequitur logicam similem augmentō, sed est technicē phaenomenon distīnctum.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Perfectum āctīvum λύω: λέλυκα, λέλυκας, λέλυκε(ν) // λελύκαμεν, λελύκατε, λελύκασι(ν). Īnfīnītīvus: λελυκέναι.
  • Reduplicātiō (cōnsonāns+ε, vel allongātiō vōcālis) est signum characteristicum, praesēns in OMNĪ fōrmā perfectī — dīversa ab augmentō.
  • Sēnsus perfectī: status praesēns resultāns ex āctiōne praeteritā complētā (“habeō solūtum”), nōn sōlum ēventum praeteritum simplex (aoristus, “solvī”).
  • Discrīmen perfectī/aoristī parallēlum discrīminī Anglicō “have solved” vs. “solved”.
