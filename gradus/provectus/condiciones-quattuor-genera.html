Gradus V · Provectus

Condiciōnēs: Quattuor Genera

Simplex (factum), irreālis (contrā factum), futūra vīvida (ἐάν+coni.), futūra minus vīvida (εἰ+opt.) — systēma completum condiciōnum Graecārum.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Condiciō Graeca cōnsistit ex duābus partibus: prōtasis ("sī"-clausula, cum εἰ/ἐάν) et apodosis (clausula prīncipālis/resultātūs). Graecum distinguit quattuor genera condiciōnum secundum quantum probābilis/reālis est condiciō ipsa — systēma multō magis fīnum quam Latīnum (quod habet trēs genera similia sed nōn identica).

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Distinguere quattuor genera condiciōnum Graecārum per fōrmam prōtasis ("sī"-clausulae) et apodosis (clausulae prīncipālis/resultātūs)
  • Cōnstruere condiciōnem simplicem (factum): εἰ + indicātīvus, indicātīvus
  • Cōnstruere condiciōnem irreālem (contrā factum): εἰ + indicātīvus praeteritī, ἄν + indicātīvus praeteritī
  • Cōnstruere condiciōnēs futūrās (vīvida: ἐάν + coniūnctīvus, futūrum indicātīvum; minus vīvida: εἰ + optātīvus, ἄν + optātīvus)

Explicatio

Explicatio

Discrīmen inter quattuor genera nōn est sōlum tempus chronologicum, sed gradus probābilitātis/reālitātis ā vīsū locūtōris: factum indubitābile (simplex), factum negātum contrā reālitātem (irreālis), possibilitās probābilis futūra (vīvida), possibilitās remōtior/hypothetica futūra (minus vīvida).

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Cōnspectus: Quattuor Genera

GenusPrōtasis (εἰ...)Apodosis
1. Simplex (factum)εἰ + indicātīvus (quōlibet tempore)indicātīvus (sine ἄν)
2. Irreālis (contrā factum)εἰ + indicātīvus praeteritī (imperf./aor.)ἄν + indicātīvus praeteritī
3. Futūra vīvidaἐάν (=εἰ+ἄν) + coniūnctīvusfutūrum indicātīvum (sine ἄν)
4. Futūra minus vīvidaεἰ + optātīvusἄν + optātīvus

b) Exempla Applicāta

1. Simplex: εἰ τοῦτο λέγει, ἁμαρτάνει. “Sī hoc dīcit, errat” [neutrāle, sine implicātiōne dē vēritāte]. 2. Irreālis (praesēns): εἰ τοῦτο ἔλεγεν, ἡμάρτανεν ἄν. “Sī hoc dīceret [nunc, sed nōn dīcit], errāret”. Irreālis (praeteritum): εἰ τοῦτο εἶπεν, ἥμαρτεν ἄν. “Sī hoc dīxisset [sed nōn dīxit], errāsset”. 3. Vīvida: ἐὰν τοῦτο λέγῃ, ἁμαρτήσεται. “Sī hoc dīcet [probābiliter], errābit”. 4. Minus vīvida: εἰ τοῦτο λέγοι, ἁμαρτάνοι ἄν. “Sī hoc fortasse dīceret, errāret [hypothetice]”.

c) Nōtae Praecipuae

ἐάν (crasis ex εἰ+ἄν) est REQUĪSĪTUS cum coniūnctīvō in prōtasī — coniūnctīvus in clausulā subōrdinātā generāliter requīrit ἄν additum ad particulam intrōductōriam (patrōnus quem posteā vidēbis etiam in clausulīs relātīvīs/temporālibus indefinītīs). Ἄν in apodosī (genera 2 et 4) est ESSENTIĀLE — sine eō, indicātīvus/optātīvus perderent sēnsum condiciōnālem. Genus 1 (simplex) et genus 3 (vīvida) apodosis NUMQUAM habent ἄν.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • εἰ σοφός ἐστιν, εὖ λέγει. — Sī sapiēns est, bene loquitur. (1, simplex)
  • εἰ σοφὸς ἦν, εὖ ἂν ἔλεγεν. — Sī sapiēns esset, bene loquerētur. (2, irreālis praesēns)
  • εἰ ταῦτα ἐποίησεν, εὖ ἂν ἔπραξεν. — Sī haec fēcisset, bene sē habuisset. (2, irreālis praeteritum)
  • ἐὰν σοφὸς ᾖ, εὖ ἐρεῖ. — Sī sapiēns erit, bene loquētur. (3, vīvida)
  • εἰ σοφὸς εἴη, εὖ ἂν λέγοι. — Sī fortasse sapiēns esset, bene loquerētur. (4, minus vīvida)
  • εἰ μὴ ἦλθες, οὐκ ἂν εἶδες τὸν βασιλέα. — Sī nōn vēnissēs, nōn vīdissēs rēgem. (2, negātīvum, μή in prōtasī, οὐκ in apodosī)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumAdhibēre εἰ (nōn ἐάν) cum coniūnctīvō in prōtasī, omittendō ἄν necessārium.

RectiusAdhibēre semper ἐάν (εἰ+ἄν combīnāta) cum coniūnctīvō in prōtasī condiciōnālī.

Coniūnctīvus in prōtasī requīrit ἄν obligātōriē — cōnstrūctiō *εἰ + coniūnctīvus sine ἄν nōn exsistit in Atticō classicō.

VitiumOmittere ἄν in apodosī generum 2 et 4 (irreālis, minus vīvida), scrībendo indicātīvum/optātīvum sōlum.

RectiusIncludere semper ἄν in apodosī generum 2 et 4 — sine eō, sēnsus condiciōnālis perditur omnīnō.

Ἄν in apodosī est signum morphologicum obligātōrium horum generum, distinguēns eōs ā simplicī affirmātiōne.

VitiumCōnfundere negātiōnem in prōtasī (semper μή, in omnibus quattuor generibus) cum negātiōne apodosis (variābilis secundum modum).

RectiusRecordārī prōtasis semper adhibet μή (etiam cum indicātīvō! — exceptiō speciālis ad rēgulam generālem οὐ/μή), apodosis sequitur rēgulam normālem prō modō suō.

Prōtasis condiciōnālis est ūna ex paucīs cōnstrūctiōnibus ubi μή adhibētur cum indicātīvō, contrā rēgulam generālem (Gradū II).

Summarium

Recognitio

  • 1. Simplex: εἰ + indic., indic. (sine ἄν) — factum neutrāle.
  • 2. Irreālis: εἰ + indic. praeteritī, ἄν + indic. praeteritī — contrā factum.
  • 3. Futūra vīvida: ἐάν + coni., fut. indic. (sine ἄν in apodosī) — probābile.
  • 4. Futūra minus vīvida: εἰ + opt., ἄν + opt. — hypotheticum/remōtius.
  • Ἐάν (=εἰ+ἄν) requīsītum cum coniūnctīvō; ἄν in apodosī essentiāle prō generibus 2/4; prōtasis semper μή.
