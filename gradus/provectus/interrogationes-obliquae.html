Gradus V · Provectus

Interrogātiōnēs Oblīquae

Quaestiōnēs indirēctae: rēgula sequentiae applicāta (indicātīvus/coniūnctīvus retentus vs. optātīvus oblīquus); εἰ prō quaestiōnibus sī/nōn; ὅστις ut interrogātīvum oblīquum.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Interrogātiō oblīqua ("rogāvī QUIS vēnisset", nōn quaestiō dīrēcta "quis vēnit?") sequitur rēgulam sequentiae iam plēnē nōtam (Gradū V): post verbum rogandī PRĪMĀRIUM, modus quaestiōnis dīrēctae originālis plērumque RETINĒTUR; post verbum rogandī SECUNDĀRIUM, MODUS FIT OPTĀTĪVUS ("optātīvus oblīquus") — patrōnus IDENTICUS illī iam vīsō in clausulīs fīnālibus.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Cōnstruere interrogātiōnem oblīquam applicandō rēgulam sequentiae (Gradū V): modus retentus sequentiā prīmāriā, optātīvus oblīquus sequentiā secundāriā
  • Adhibēre εἰ prō quaestiōnibus indirēctīs "sī/num" (quaestiōnēs sine verbō interrogātīvō explicitō)
  • Recognōscere ὅστις, ἥτις, ὅτι (compositum ex ὅς + τις) ut prōnōmen interrogātīvum oblīquum frequēns, alternātīvum τίς
  • Distinguere quaestiōnem dīrēctam (cum τίς; signum ;) ā quaestiōne indirēctā (sine ; propriō, intrā clausulam maiōrem)

Explicatio

Explicatio

Duo genera interrogātiōnum oblīquārum: (1) cum verbō interrogātīvō (τίς, ποῦ, πῶς, quae iam nōta ex Gradū II), et (2) sine verbō interrogātīvō, quaestiō "sī/num" — hīc Graecum adhibet εἰ (litt. "sī", homographum condiciōnālis, sed fūnctiōne dīversā hīc).

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Sequentia Prīmāria: Modus Retentus

Post verbum rogandī PRAESĒNS/FUTŪRUM: modus quaestiōnis dīrēctae originālis manet (indicātīvus sī erat indicātīvus; coniūnctīvus dēlīberātīvus sī erat dēlīberātīvus): ἐρωτῶ τίς ἐστιν. "Rogō quis sit" (litt. "quis est", indicātīvus retentus, ā τίς ἐστιν; dīrēctā). ἐρωτῶ τί ποιῶμεν. "Rogō quid faciāmus" (dēlīberātīvus retentus).

b) Sequentia Secundāria: Optātīvus Oblīquus

Post verbum rogandī PRAETERITUM: modus fit OPTĀTĪVUS, indepēndenter ā modō quaestiōnis dīrēctae originālis: ἠρώτησα τίς εἴη. "Rogāvī quis esset" (opt., ā τίς ἐστιν; indicātīvā orīgināliter). ἠρώτησεν εἰ ἔλθοι ὁ βασιλεύς. "Rogāvit num rēx vēnisset/venīret" (quaestiō "sī/num", ā εἰ + optātīvus).

c) Ὅστις: Interrogātīvum Oblīquum Alternātīvum

ὅστις, ἥτις, ὅτι (litt. "quīcumque", compositum ex relātīvō ὅς + indēfīnītō τις, AMBABUS partibus dēclīnātīs: gen. οὗτινος, etc.) frequenter substituit τίς in interrogātiōnibus oblīquīs, praesertim apud auctōrēs Atticōs classicōs: οὐκ οἶδα ὅστις ἐστίν. "Nesciō quis sit." Nōtā: fōrma neutra ὅτι est identica scrīptūrā (sed dīversa accentū/contextū) coniūnctiōnī ὅτι "quod, quia" — cōnfūsiō possibilis, ēvītanda per contextum.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • ἐρωτῶ τίς ἐστιν. — Rogō quis sit. (prīmāria, indicātīvus retentus)
  • ἠρώτησα τίς εἴη. — Rogāvī quis esset. (secundāria, optātīvus oblīquus)
  • οὐκ οἶδα εἰ ἀληθές ἐστιν. — Nesciō num vērum sit. (εἰ, quaestiō "sī")
  • ἠπόρει ὅ τι ποιοῖ. — Dubitābat quid faceret. (secundāria, ὅτι neutrum interrogātīvum, nōn coniūnctiō)
  • ἐρωτῶσιν ὅστις ἦλθεν. — Rogant quis vēnerit. (ὅστις prō τίς, prīmāria)
  • οὐκ ᾔδειν εἰ σοφὸς εἴη ἢ μή. — Nesciēbam num sapiēns esset annōn. (εἰ...ἢ μή, alternātīva)

Errores Communes

Cave

VitiumRetinēre semper indicātīvum in interrogātiōne oblīquā, ignōrandō rēgulam sequentiae post verbum praeteritum.

RectiusApplicāre optātīvum oblīquum post verbum rogandī SECUNDĀRIUM, exāctē sīcut in clausulīs fīnālibus/condiciōnālibus.

Interrogātiō oblīqua sequitur eandem rēgulam sequentiae systēmaticam ac cēterae clausulae subōrdinātae.

VitiumAdhibēre ἵνα vel ὅπως prō quaestiōne indirēctā "sī/num", cōnfundendō cum clausulā fīnālī.

RectiusAdhibēre εἰ specificē prō quaestiōnibus sine verbō interrogātīvō explicitō ("num...").

Discrīmen syntacticum clārum: ἵνα/ὅπως intrōdūcunt propositum; εἰ intrōdūcit quaestiōnem indirēctam sine interrogātīvō.

VitiumCōnfundere ὅτι interrogātīvum (neutrum ὅστις, "quid") cum ὅτι coniūnctiōne ("quod, quia"), propter fōrmam scrīptūrae identicam.

RectiusDistinguere per contextum syntacticum: ὅ τι (duo verba, saepe scrīpta sēparātim) est interrogātīvum; ὅτι (ūnum verbum) est coniūnctiō.

Ambiguitās orthographica reālis, resolvenda per convention scrīptūrae (spatium) et contextum.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Interrogātiō oblīqua sequitur rēgulam sequentiae: modus retentus (prīmāria) vs. optātīvus oblīquus (secundāria).
  • Εἰ intrōdūcit quaestiōnem indirēctam "sī/num", sine verbō interrogātīvō explicitō.
  • Ὅστις, ἥτις, ὅτι (= ὅς + τις, ambābus partibus dēclīnātīs) substituit frequenter τίς in interrogātiōne oblīquā.
  • Ὅ τι interrogātīvum ≠ ὅτι coniūnctiō ("quod, quia") — ambiguitās orthographica, resolvenda per contextum.
